  <div class="case-study-info">
    <h3>Bridging the gap between Design, Engineering, and Inclusive UX.</h3>
    <p>Learn more about how I approach <a href="/accessibility/index">this site's accessibility</a>.</p>
    <p>I am a Senior UX Designer and Accessibility Specialist with over a decade of experience building digital products that don't just meet compliance standards but exceed user expectations. Most recently, I served as a Lead at Ad Hoc, where I architected accessibility processes for the federal government (Healthcare.gov / Medicare.gov) to ensure critical services are available to everyone.</p>
    <p>I specialize in Design Operations for Accessibility—creating the frameworks, annotation systems, and "Shift-Left" mentalities that allow cross-functional teams to scale inclusive design without sacrificing velocity.</p>
    <h3>The "Why" Behind My Work</h3>
    <ul role="list">
      <li role="listitem"><b>Deterministic Handoff:</b> Providing engineers with clear, code-ready accessibility intent to eliminate guesswork.</i>
      <li role="listitem"><b>Operational Scale:</b> Moving away from "one-off" audits toward repeatable, automated systems.</i>
      <li role="listitem"><b>Radical Inclusion:</b> Designing for the "edges" to create a better experience for the center.</i>
    </ul>
